Transparency tool for detecting bias in selection committees.

## Purpose

Equity Lens helps selection committees detect potential bias patterns (gender disparity, institutional concentration, familiarity bias) before final decisions are made. It does not rank candidates, store individual votes, or replace human judgment.

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Login as a committee member
3. Add candidate information
4. Click "Run Bias Check" to see the dashboard
5. Committee members vote privately
6. Chairperson sees aggregated results (once they log in as Chairperson).

## Features

- Privacy-preserving (no individual votes stored)
- Flags: Gender disparity, institutional concentration, familiarity bias
- Calculates m-quotient (h-index ÷ years active)
- GDPR/DSGVO compliant
