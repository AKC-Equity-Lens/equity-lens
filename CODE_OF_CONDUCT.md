# Code of Conduct

EquityLens exists to make selection processes fairer. A project with that aim
cannot tolerate conduct in its own community that mirrors what it is trying to
surface elsewhere.

## What we expect

- Engage with arguments, evidence and code — not with the person making them.
- Assume the people here have different experience of selection processes than
  you do, including experience of being excluded by them. Take it seriously.
- Disagree openly. Criticism of an indicator, a threshold or a design decision is
  the point of this project, and robust technical disagreement is welcome.
- Accept that a contribution may be declined for reasons of fairness or privacy
  rather than technical quality.
- The tool is only productive and useful if you do not manipulate the data, you are entering.
  Refrain pre-designing bias.

## What is unacceptable

- Harassment, demeaning comments, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct, including on the basis of gender, gender
  identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, ethnicity, religion,
  nationality, age, career stage or institutional affiliation.
- Publishing anyone's private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.
- Using this project's data or tools to build a case against an individual.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and wherever someone
is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to **akc@dpg-physik.de**. Reports are read by the AKC
working group and handled confidentially. If a report concerns a maintainer, it
can be addressed to the DPG working group directly rather than to the project.

We will respond to reports, and we may remove content, decline contributions, or
exclude a participant where that is warranted. We will explain the basis for any
such decision to the person affected.

## Attribution

This code of conduct draws on the conventions established by the Contributor
Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org), adapted for this project.
